# Elisha Otis, safety elevator (21 October 2026)
Facts used: Britannica (born 1811, bedstead factory Yonkers, May 1854 Crystal Palace demonstration with rope cut, 23 March 1857 first passenger safety elevator at E.V. Haughwout store, died 8 April 1861 aged 49); Encyclopedia.com (buildings usually no higher than 4 storeys, sales 15 in 1855 and 27 in 1856, American Institute Fair 1854); New England Historical Society (platform dropped 2 feet, "All safe gentlemen, all safe"); Otis website (about 2.5 billion people a day, company claim; another source says 2.4 billion).
Facts avoided because sources disagree: 1853 versus 1854 for the demonstration date, the $100 Barnum fee and $122.71 balance (single source), height of the platform (unstated), elevators installed by 1861 (unstated).
