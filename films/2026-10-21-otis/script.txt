New York, 1854.
Buildings rarely passed 4 floors, because people would not climb more.

Elisha Otis, a bedstead mechanic, built a platform that locks if the rope breaks.

An assistant cut the rope with an axe.
The platform dropped 2 feet and stopped.
All safe, gentlemen, all safe.
