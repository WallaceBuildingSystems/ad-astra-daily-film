# Willis Carrier, air conditioning, 1902 (researched 16 October 2026 from pages opened in this session)

Sources opened:
- Carrier Global history, Invention of Air Conditioning: https://www.carrier.com/us/en/history/invention-of-air-conditioning/ (company history, cites the 1902 design date and the plant)
- Hagley Museum and Library, Willis Carrier: https://hagley.org/node/779 (institutional source, confirms 17 July 1902 drawings, Buffalo Forge, patent 808,897 of 1906, left Buffalo Forge 1915)
- United States Energy Information Administration, Today in Energy 52558: https://www.eia.gov/todayinenergy/detail.php?id=52558 (88 percent of United States households used air conditioning in 2020, Residential Energy Consumption Survey)

Facts used:
- 1902, Sackett and Wilhelms Lithography and Printing Company, Brooklyn: humidity warped paper, colours misaligned, edges wavy, ink dried slowly, a magazine schedule at risk.
- Carrier, a Cornell graduate about a year out, aged 25, engineer at Buffalo Forge, given the job by J. Irvine Lyle.
- Chilled coils, fans, ducts, humidistats; plant held at 55 percent humidity year-round, 70 and 80 degrees Fahrenheit (21 and 27 degrees Celsius, converted).
- 17 July 1902 drawings; installed late summer 1902.
- Patent 808,897 issued 2 January 1906, Apparatus for Treating Air.
- Carrier Engineering Corporation incorporated 26 June 1915.
- 88 percent of United States households use air conditioning, 2020.

Not used: Carrier company claims about later products. Failures: no documented failed attempts found in the sources, so the film has no failed-attempts list.
